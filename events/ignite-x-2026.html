IGNITE X 2026

TechShake's 10th flagship innovation conference, during Philippine Startup Week 2026

nov 10, 2026 · min read · joana daphne sy

“AI isn’t a ‘strategic advantage.’ Your choices of how to use it are.”

An intelligent ASEAN future

On November 10, I attended IGNITE X 2026, the 10th edition of TechShake's flagship innovation conference, held during Philippine Startup Week 2026 at the Quezon City MICE Center. It showed me how fast the Philippine and ASEAN startup scene is moving right now.

The day was packed with strategic partnership signings, panel discussions and the Startup World Cup Philippines Regional Finale, and every session came back to the same goal of shaping an intelligent ASEAN future. Watching founders pitch their products on the main stage made the concepts we study in class feel much more practical.

On the exhibit floor

Walking around the exhibitor booths showed me that real innovation comes down to how you apply technology to everyday gaps.

I had a great chat with the team behind synco, an all-in-one digital system that helps Filipino MSMEs manage sales, inventory, HR and BIR-ready records. I also stopped by Akaiin, a healthtech platform that helps families support their aging parents through shared care circles and trusted companions. Seeing local builders take on real problems like business operations and elder care was really encouraging.

Why I chose this field

Events like Philippine Startup Week remind me why I chose this field in the first place. Hearing from founders and seeing their work up close makes the whole experience worth it. Big thanks to TechShake, the government partners, exhibitors and speakers for putting together such a memorable event.
