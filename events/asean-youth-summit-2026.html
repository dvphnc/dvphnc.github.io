ASEAN Campus Connect & Youth Leadership Summit

Around 300 student leaders from 53 institutions, and one lesson about listening

sep 9, 2026 · min read · joana daphne sy

“We grow as leaders not when we hold the mic, but when we make space to listen.”

Leadership starts with intent

On September 9, I joined the ASEAN Campus Connect and Youth Leadership Summit at U.P. Town Center, hosted by CHED NCR. Around 300 student leaders from 53 higher education institutions filled the room, each carrying the work of their own campus.

Real leadership always comes down to our intentions. Titles and programs matter less than why we take them on and who we are doing it for.

Listening first

The sessions kept returning to the same idea: collaboration works when we listen before we speak. Building trust, letting the whole team own the work, and treating diversity as something we approach with curiosity and empathy all start from making space for other voices.

Only the beginning

I left with a clearer picture of the kind of leader I want to be for the student communities I serve. Bringing this vision to life is only the beginning.
