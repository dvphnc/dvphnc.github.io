Overview

A full-stack Barangay Management System for Barangay New Era: filing blotter cases, issuing clearances, tracking business permits, and letting residents request documents online. A group project for Integrative Programming and Technologies at New Era University, with every feature grounded in real barangay workflows.

The problem

Barangay offices still run on paper folders, physical logbooks and manual signatures. This system replaces all of that: one place for resident records, case management, document issuance and real-time status tracking, built for the staff who use it every day.

What it does

  • Resident and household management
  • Document issuance with PDF certificates
  • Blotter case management
  • Business permit tracking, with QR codes for public verification
  • A resident portal with a live request tracker
  • Appointments and queue management
  • Officials, staff and committees
  • Reports and analytics with Excel export
  • System administration: roles, activity log, backup and restore

My role

I served as Scrum Master and Full-Stack Developer, leading sprints, managing the backlog, and owning the end-to-end technical implementation.

  • Facilitated sprint planning, daily stand-ups and retrospectives
  • Designed the full database schema: 20+ tables
  • Built every controller, model, migration and route from scratch
  • Wrote the PDF certificate pipeline with dynamic resident data
  • Built real-time portal tracking with queued email notifications
  • Designed the whole interface from scratch, with no UI kit

How it’s built

A Laravel 12 application that runs fully offline: all assets are served locally, so no Node.js is needed. Server-side DataTables power every module, with filtering, searching and multi-format export.
