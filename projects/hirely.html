Overview

HIRELY is a full-stack HR Management System built to modernize how teams handle recruitment, employee records and HR workflows.

The problem

Most HR tools are bloated and expensive. HIRELY keeps it focused: track applicants, manage employees, handle leave requests, and get a clear view of your workforce from one clean dashboard.

What it does

  • Recruitment pipeline for applicants
  • Employee directory and profiles
  • Role-based access control
  • Leave requests and approvals
  • Dashboard analytics

My role

I served as both Scrum Master and UI/UX Designer, two roles that ended up being more connected than I expected.

  • Ran daily standups, sprint planning and retrospectives
  • Tracked milestones and surfaced blockers early
  • Led the design process from lo-fi sketches to Figma prototypes
  • Designed interfaces around real HR tasks, not assumed ones
  • Ran usability checks with the team before handoff

Result

It started as a school project under a tight two-month sprint and turned into something I genuinely care about. Built Agile, shipped on time.
